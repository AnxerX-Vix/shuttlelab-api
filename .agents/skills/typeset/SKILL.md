# Typeset (Impeccable Design Suite)

Clean, readable, and athletic typography standards:

1. **Hierarchy Scales**:
   - Page Title: `text-xl font-extrabold tracking-tight text-slate-900`
   - Section Title: `text-sm font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-slate-400`
   - Product / Item Name: `text-sm font-bold text-slate-900 leading-snug`
   - Numeric & Spec Value: `text-xs font-semibold text-slate-800`
   - Badge & Tag: `text-[10px] font-bold uppercase tracking-wide`

2. **Rhythm**:
   - Headings line-height `1.15` - `1.25`.
   - Body & descriptions line-height `1.5` - `1.6`.
