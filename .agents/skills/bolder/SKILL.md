# Bolder (Impeccable Design Suite)

Break out of boring SaaS defaults and inject athletic energy:

1. **Strong Brand & Editorial Accents**:
   - High-contrast text colors (`text-slate-900` over `bg-slate-50`).
   - Rich court-green accents (`bg-emerald-600`, `text-emerald-700`).
   - Sharp, technical typography for product specs (uppercase labels, tracked font weights).

2. **Distinctive Card Geometries**:
   - Vary card layouts: mix compact editorial rows with clean product hero imagery.
   - Use crisp, high-precision border definitions rather than muddy card shadows.
