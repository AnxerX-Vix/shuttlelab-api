# Critique (Impeccable Design Suite)

Analyze interface designs with an expert design director eye:

1. **Hierarchy & Rhythm**:
   - Is visual hierarchy clear at a single glance?
   - Does typography lead the eye naturally from primary to secondary to tertiary?
   - Are spacing and margins proportional and consistent?

2. **Visual Clutter & Template Tells**:
   - Look out for "uniform white cards stacked endlessly" (AI slop card grid syndrome).
   - Flag unnecessary marketing banners, loud colored callouts, and meaningless decorative badges.
   - Demand sharp, editorial sports directory feel instead of generic SaaS template.

3. **Touch Ergonomics & Proportions**:
   - Are touch targets on mobile at least 44x44px?
   - Does the bottom navigation feel well-proportioned, or is it bloated and taking up too much vertical space?
